Alelus

C++

C++20 random number generation library with two generators sharing one interface: FastRNG (xoshiro256**), for simulation, Monte Carlo and games, and CryptoRNG, a cryptographically secure generator based on ChaCha20 (RFC 8439).

Highlights

  • Unbiased sampling: Lemire's rejection method, Fisher–Yates shuffling and Vose's alias method for weighted choice.
  • Reproducible results across compilers and operating systems, and non-overlapping streams for threads.
  • Validated against reference vectors (RFC 8439) and with chi-square tests on the distributions.

  • ChaCha20
