Syllabi

Courses

Summary of the syllabi of the courses I have taught.

Linear AlgebraÁlgebra Linear

The language of matrices, linear systems and linear maps, a foundation for almost every quantitative discipline.

Syllabus

  • Matrices: operations, invertible matrices, row echelon form and rank
  • Systems of linear equations: Gaussian and Gauss–Jordan elimination; Cramer systems
  • Determinants: properties, Laplace expansion and adjugate matrix
  • Vector spaces ℝn: linear independence, subspaces, bases and dimension
  • Eigenvalues and eigenvectors; diagonalization
  • Linear maps from ℝn to ℝm: matrix, kernel and image

Applications

  • Computer graphics
  • Data analysis
  • Markov chains
  • Optimization

Topics in MathematicsTópicos de Matemática

Matrix algebra and multivariable calculus, oriented towards environmental and Earth sciences.

Syllabus

  • Matrices, determinants and systems of linear equations
  • Limits and continuity of functions of several real variables
  • Partial and total differentiation
  • Local and constrained extrema
  • Multiple integration: areas and volumes
  • Line and surface integrals; Green's and Stokes' theorems

Applications

  • Environmental modeling
  • Areas & volumes
  • Fields & fluxes
  • Optimization

Topics in Probability and Number TheoryTópicos de Probabilidades e Teoria de Números

Two essential areas for computing: probabilistic reasoning and the arithmetic of the integers, the basis of cryptography.

Syllabus

  • Probability: axioms, conditioning and independence
  • Probability distributions, moments and stochastic convergence
  • Parametric models, parameter estimation and data modeling
  • Divisibility, greatest common divisor and the Euclidean algorithm
  • Prime numbers and the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic
  • Diophantine equations and linear congruences

Applications

  • Cryptography (RSA)
  • Simulation
  • Algorithm analysis
  • Data modeling

StatisticsEstatística

Statistical methods to compare groups and relate variables, applied to research in psychology.

Syllabus

  • t-tests for independent and paired samples
  • One-way ANOVA and repeated-measures ANOVA
  • Simple linear regression
  • Non-parametric Mann–Whitney and Kruskal–Wallis tests
  • Non-parametric Wilcoxon and Friedman tests

Applications

  • Psychology research
  • Experimental design
  • Group comparisons
  • Data analysis

CalculusCálculo

The mathematics of change and accumulation, developing both computational skill and rigorous reasoning.

Syllabus

  • Real functions: limits, continuity, inverse, trigonometric and hyperbolic functions
  • Derivatives, Taylor polynomials and L'Hôpital's rule
  • Antiderivatives: integration by parts, by substitution and of rational functions
  • Riemann integral and fundamental theorems of calculus; improper integrals
  • Areas and arc lengths
  • Numerical series and power series; Taylor series

Applications

  • Modeling rates of change
  • Physics & engineering
  • Optimization
  • Numerical methods
